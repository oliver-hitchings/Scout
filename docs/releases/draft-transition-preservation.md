# Preserve reviewed draft work when switching

Starting a different reviewed CV or letter draft now requires explicit discard
of an existing unsaved preview and its human edits. Cancelling retains the text.
Generation and saving block draft replacement until they finish. A pending save
also locks editing, duplicate saves and discard actions.

Synthetic browser regressions exercise both role and draft-type switches,
declined and accepted discard, delayed generation and delayed saving.
