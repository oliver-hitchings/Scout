# Getting started with Scout

Scout finds jobs for you to review. It keeps a separate private folder for your CV, preferences and results. It never sends an application or cover letter.

1. Install Scout using the guide for [Windows](INSTALL_WINDOWS.md), [macOS](INSTALL_MACOS.md) or [Linux](INSTALL_LINUX.md), then open it.
2. Choose **Set up Scout for the first time**. Keep the suggested workspace folder for your own use. Each other person needs their own workspace; see the [workspace guide](REPOSITORY_LAYOUT.md).
3. Choose an AI service you already use and follow its sign-in or connection instructions. Scout shows whether it is ready. You do not need two services. See [AI setup](AI_SETUP.md) if none is available.
4. Answer the questions about work you would consider, where you could work and any dealbreakers. Check the proposed language, currency and time zone. Leave pay blank if you are unsure. No country is selected for optional Adzuna searches until you choose one.
5. Choose **Background watch** if you only want exceptional matches, or **Active job hunt** if you are looking now. Background watch is the default. Automatic scans stay off until you enable a schedule.
6. Import your CV. Scout reads selectable-text PDF, Word, Markdown and plain text files. Check that the preview contains your words. Scanned pictures of a CV need text recognition first.
7. Choose **Generate proposal**, read the proposed CV and preferences, and approve them only when the facts are correct. Review and publish the search rules, then choose **Run first scan now**.

When the scan finishes, open **Jobs** and read a result. Scout shows what it found and what it could assess; a failed source or missing advert fact is shown as a limitation. Shortlist a role you want to consider. A zero-result scan can be a valid result: check your preferences before widening them.

Use **Good fit** or **Poor fit** to explain a result. Feedback can stay with that job, or create a ranking suggestion for your review in **Settings → Feedback & learning**. A suggestion does not take effect until you publish it.

When Scout updates a list of jobs, it keeps an open card and unfinished feedback
for a job that is still in that list. Changed job details update without clearing
your explanation. Select **Save feedback** when you are ready to keep it.

From a shortlisted job, create a CV or cover-letter draft. With a local model, Scout selects confirmed CV passages for an editable preview; confirm your review before saving it. You can also start this reviewed flow from **CV → Review CV options**. Edit saved drafts in **CV**, then render and download a PDF. Guided CV and cover-letter PDFs are labelled **DRAFT** and Scout never sends them.

To keep a CV from your notes app connected, open **CV → Living CV**. Scout checks the local file for changes and asks you to approve the diff. Read [Living CV and drafts](LIVING_CV.md) for the file format and version history.

You can close Scout and come back later. Uninstalling the application keeps your separate workspace. For backup, phone access and optional scheduled scans, see the [full quick start](QUICK_START.md). If something does not work, start with [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
